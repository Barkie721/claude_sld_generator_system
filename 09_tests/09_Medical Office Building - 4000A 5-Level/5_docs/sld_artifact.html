Emergency system — NEC 700

Life safety loads only: egress and exit lighting, fire alarm, generator accessories. Elevators and everything else stay on normal power. The emergency gear sits in a 2-hour room apart from the main electrical room and rises in its own closet stack.

  1. GEN-1 100 kW / 125 kVA diesel, outdoor enclosure, NFPA 110 Level 1, Type 10. Breaker 175 A.
  2. E4MDP-1-A 400 A emergency board; main 175 A LSI, ECB-01 175 A.
  3. E4A-1-A 225 A 3-pole ATS. Normal source CB-14, emergency source ECB-01.
  4. E4P-1-A 225 A emergency distribution panel (EER, L1). Serves L1–L2 egress lighting.
  5. E4L-3-A 100 A (L3) → E4L-5-A 100 A (L5).
  6. E4T-1-A 30 kVA → E2P-1-A 100 A MCB, 208Y/120 V (fire alarm control).

What the drawing shows differently from the brief

  • An emergency board, E4MDP-1-A, sits between GEN-1 and the ATS. The layout engine anchors the emergency sheet on a switchboard lineup.
  • DS-IMG, the imaging disconnect, is not drawn because the engine has no disconnect symbol yet. It is still in the field, on the imaging corridor wall.
  • Transformer chains draw just above the switchboard whatever their floor, so 2P-3-A, 2P-5-A and the site EV panels carry their level in the designation rather than a level badge.
