## SERVICE
2500 kVA utility pad-mount transformer, 13.2 kV primary → 480Y/277V secondary,
3-phase, 4-wire, 5.75% Z, delta-wye, utility-owned.
Utility feeder (F-002, to 4MDP-1-A line side):
  4 sets, 4"C EMT, 4#750 kcmil CU + #1/0 CU GND, +2 spare conduits.

## BUILDING
3-story acute-care hospital, 121,000 SF, plus occupied roof level (mechanical
penthouse). Main service switchboard in L1 electrical room.
Level lines: Roof (R), Level 3 (3), Level 2 (2), Level 1 (1).
