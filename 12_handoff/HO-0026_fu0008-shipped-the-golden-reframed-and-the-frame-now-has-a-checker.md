## 3. 🚩 The fix, and the one decision inside it that widened scope

The old step 1 consumed each `<g transform="translate(…)">` and bounded it by a ±r square where
**r was the largest number anywhere in that group's markup string**. A pennant reading `41,837A`
claimed **±837 px**; a digit-free one claimed **±700** off `font-weight="700"`; true geometry is
about ±100. The `< 10000` clamp was the author's own tell.

Steps 1-3 now live in **`_mmeScan(markup, m, ext)`** — the same text/shape math, walked under a 2×3
affine. A transformed `<g>` recurses with `m · T`; nested groups compose; `rect`/`text` emit four
corners instead of two (a no-op at the identity, a bound under rotation).

**Three deliberate widenings past "step 1 only", each argued:**

1. **Balanced slicing instead of the non-greedy `</g>`.** The old regex stopped at the FIRST close
   tag, so a nested group would spill its remainder into the parent frame. Zero such groups exist
   today; the recursion would have created the hazard it was meant to remove.
2. **Any `<g>` carrying a transform, not just `translate(`-prefixed ones.** A `rotate()`-first group
   was silently measured in local coordinates before. Zero exist today.
3. **`transform` on shape/text elements composes too.** *A child transform ignored is precisely
   "the recursion missed a child".* Two such elements exist per sheet (a `rotate(30,345,4890)` arc
   and its line); both are interior, so honouring them changes no extreme.

> ⚠️ **All three are INERT on today's corpus, which means the suite alone cannot prove them right.**
> That is exactly the objection this repo would raise, so they are covered by **unit tests instead
> of by luck** — `behavior_test.mjs` §12-13 exercises nesting, `rotate(a,cx,cy)`, mirror and uniform
> scale against hand-derived numbers.

**The identity path is bit-exact by construction**: `_mmeEmitter` returns `ext` *unwrapped* when
`m === _MME_I`, so untransformed markup never touches the matrix. Measured, not asserted — see §4.
